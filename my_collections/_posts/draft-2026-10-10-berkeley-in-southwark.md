### Third Berkeley scheme rejected 

Southwark Council unanimously rejected developer Berkeley's proposals for the development of Bermondsey Place Phases 2 and 3, at its planning committtee meeting on 6 October.  This follows Berkeley's failure to gain approval for its Borough Triangle proposals, at the [beginning of September](https://moderngov.southwark.gov.uk/ieListDocuments.aspx?CId=650&MId=8449&Ver=4) and Aylesham Centre applications, [in July 2025](https://35percent.org/2025/07/16/aylesham-centre-planning-application-rejected.html)
